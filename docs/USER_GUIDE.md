# Sift User Guide

Welcome to **Sift**, your intelligent news curator designed to eliminate clickbait, emotional manipulation, and information overload.

---

## 📱 Core Features & Screens

### 1. Home Feed (`HomeScreen`)
- **Signal-Heavy Curation**: View prioritized top stories scored for information density vs. sensationalism.
- **Swipe-to-Dismiss**: Easily swipe away articles you aren't interested in to keep your feed clean.
- **Audio Digest Floating Bar**: Instantly launch text-to-speech audio playback for hands-free briefings while commuting or multitasking.

### 2. News Radar (`NewsRadarScreen`)
- **Noise & Trap Radar**: Detects and highlights trending clickbait keywords, emotional traps, and sensationalist patterns across media outlets.
- **Real-Time Analytics**: Visualizes media SNR (Signal-to-Noise Ratio) trends.

### 3. Audio Digest (`AudioDigestScreen`)
- **Queue Management**: Listen to curated daily intelligence briefings with playback speed controls (0.75x to 2.0x), skip, and pause.
- **Voice Synthesis**: Uses Android's Text-to-Speech engine to read out summaries and key takeaways.

### 4. Bookmarks (`BookmarksScreen`)
- **Offline Reading**: Save articles for permanent offline access stored securely in the local Room database.
- **Quick Search & Filter**: Search saved bookmarks by keyword or category.

### 5. Article Detail (`ArticleDetailScreen`)
- **Gemini AI Summarization**: Choose between **Flash Mode** (quick 1-minute brief) and **Deep Dive Mode** (comprehensive analysis, fact-checking, and sentiment breakdown).
- **Translation**: Translate articles instantly into multiple languages.
- **In-App Browser**: Read full articles without leaving the app.

### 6. Settings (`SettingsScreen`)
- **Gemini API Key**: Configure your Gemini API key for AI features.
- **Notification & Background Sync**: Toggle periodic background intelligence sync via WorkManager.
- **Language & Region**: Customize news sources and language preferences.

---

## 🛠️ Tips for Best Experience
1. **API Key Setup**: Ensure you provide a valid Google Gemini API key in Settings to unlock AI summaries and SNR scoring.
2. **Background Sync**: Enable background updates to receive morning and evening intelligence briefings automatically.
